k-cal's food data comes from:

USDA FoodData Central, https://fdc.nal.usda.gov (public domain, CC0).
Open Food Facts, https://world.openfoodfacts.org. The Open Food Facts rows in k-cal/Data/foods.sqlite and the
  foods the app downloads are a database made available under the Open Database Licence,
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ Individual contents are under the Database Contents Licence,
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/
MenuStat, chain restaurant menus for 2017-2018, from the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene through
  NYC Open Data, https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Health/DOHMH-MenuStat-Historical-/qgc5-ecnb
Canadian Nutrient File, Health Canada. Contains information licensed under the Open Government
  Licence - Canada, https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada

k-cal's meal-photo estimate uses these models, converted to Core ML by tools/meal_estimate/convert.py.

MealPlate.mlpackage
  Derived from SigLIP 2 (google/siglip2-base-patch16-384), https://huggingface.co/google/siglip2-base-patch16-384
  Copyright 2025 Google LLC, licensed under the Apache License 2.0, https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
  Changed for k-cal: the last layers were fine-tuned, new output layers were added, and the weights were quantized.
  Trained on the January Food Benchmark, https://github.com/January-ai/food-scan-benchmarks (January AI, CC BY 4.0),
  and Nutrition5k (Thames et al., CVPR 2021, CC BY 4.0), by way of the calorie-lens-data repackaging by Shalev Ben Moshe,
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/shalev396/calorie-lens-data (CC BY 4.0).

DishClassifier.mlpackage
  Derived from FoodVision Big, https://huggingface.co/shalev396/foodvision-big
  Copyright (c) 2026 Shalev Ben Moshe. MIT License. The weights were quantized.
  Trained on Food-101 (Bossard et al., ECCV 2014).
